Terms of use

Last updated November 14, 2026

Joenell Studio (“we”) runs joenellstudio.com, a platform that connects couples with independent wedding and elopement photographers in British Columbia. By using the site you agree to these terms.

What we do

We vet photographers, show their work and prices, pass booking requests to them and handle deposits. Each photographer is an independent business. The photography itself is provided by the photographer under the standard Joenell Studio booking agreement, which you sign before paying.

Booking requests

A booking request is not a booking. Your date is only held once the photographer has accepted, you have signed the agreement and your deposit is paid.

Deposits and payments

  • Each photographer sets a deposit of at least 20% of the package price.
  • Deposits are non-refundable if the couple cancels. If the photographer can't fulfil the booking, the couple gets the full deposit back.
  • Joenell Studio's 15% commission is taken from the deposit. Couples pay no booking fee.
  • The balance is due before the wedding, as set out in the agreement.

If a photographer can't fulfil a booking

Deposits are non-refundable, except when the photographer can't fulfil the agreement: then the photographer refunds the couple's full deposit. The couple can also choose another photographer on Joenell Studio for their date.

Photographers

Photographers must stay vetted (insurance, quality, on-time delivery and replies within 48 hours) and use the standard agreement for bookings made through Joenell Studio. We may remove a photographer from the platform after repeated poor reviews or broken commitments.

Photos and content

Photos on this site belong to the photographers who made them. Please don't copy them without permission. Photographers confirm they have the right to show the photos they upload.

Reviews

Only couples with a real booking can leave a review. Reviews must be honest and respectful.

Contact

Questions about these terms: hello@joenellstudio.com.
