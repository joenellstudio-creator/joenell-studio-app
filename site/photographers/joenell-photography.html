About

Hello from Joenell

Joenell Photography is a Vancouver wedding and couples photographer, and the first photographer on Joenell Studio. The style is natural and warm: gentle direction, real moments and bright, true-to-life colour.

From city hall vows to a garden wedding in autumn and a surprise proposal at Bowen Lookout, the aim is the same: photos that feel like the day felt. Coverage across Metro Vancouver and the Sea-to-Sky, and elsewhere in BC by request.

Website: joenellphotography.ca
