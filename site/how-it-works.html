How it works

Simple for couples, fair for photographers

Joenell Studio connects Vancouver couples with vetted photographers at fixed prices, and stands behind every booking.

For couples

Pick a package and date

City Hall / Elopement, Adventure Elopement or Micro-Wedding.

Choose or get matched

Request a photographer you like, or let us match you with someone who's free on your date.

Hear back within 48 hours

The photographer accepts or declines within 48 hours. If they can't, we suggest someone else.

Sign and pay the deposit

One standard agreement, signed online, and a secure card deposit (at least 20%) to hold your date.

Enjoy your day

Reminders before the wedding. If your photographer can't fulfil the booking, you get your full deposit back or choose another photographer.

Get your photos, leave a review

Your gallery arrives on time, and you can leave a verified review to help other couples.

For photographers

Apply

Send one full gallery in private, your portfolio and proof of insurance.

Get approved

After a short meeting, your profile and flipbook go live right away, with a welcome post on Instagram.

Get booked

Booking requests arrive by email. 15% is taken from the deposit only when you're booked.

FAQ

Questions and answers

What does “vetted” mean?

Before anyone joins, Joenell reviews one complete wedding or elopement gallery from them in private, looks through their portfolio, checks they carry liability insurance and meets them. Only photographers who can cover a whole day well are accepted.

How do prices work?

Each photographer lists their own packages and prices on their profile, so you see the price before you ask. Search by type (city hall, adventure elopement or micro-wedding) to compare “from” prices.

Do couples pay a booking fee?

No. You pay the photographer's package price and nothing on top.

How do I book?

Send a booking request with your date and package. The photographer replies within 48 hours. Once they accept, you get the agreement to sign online and a secure link to pay your deposit. Your date is held as soon as the deposit is paid.

How do deposits work?

Each photographer sets a deposit of at least 20% of the package price. The deposit holds your date and is non-refundable if you cancel. The balance is due before the wedding, as shown in your agreement.

What if my photographer can't make it?

If your photographer can't fulfil your booking, they refund your full deposit. You can also choose another photographer on Joenell Studio for your date.

Where do your photographers work?

Metro Vancouver and the Sea-to-Sky corridor, including Whistler. Other places in BC can be arranged with your photographer.

Who owns the photos?

Your agreement covers this in plain words: you get your edited photos to keep, print and share, and the photographer keeps the copyright.
