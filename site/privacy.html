Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: for couples, your names, contact details, wedding date, location and notes; for photographers, your application details and links.

How we use it

To arrange your booking, we share a couple's request with the photographer it's for (or the photographers we match you with). We use photographers' applications to review them. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

Form entries are stored securely with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. We may use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits without tracking you across sites.

Private galleries

Full galleries sent with a photographer application are only used for vetting and are never published.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@joenellstudio.com.
