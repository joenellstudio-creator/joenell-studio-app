For photographers

Get booked. Pay only when you are.

Joenell Studio sends Vancouver couples to vetted wedding and elopement photographers. Joining is free. When a couple books you through the platform, Joenell Studio keeps a 15% commission. That's it.

Apply to join

Free to join

No monthly fee and nothing to pay to be seen. 15% of the package price, only on bookings made through Joenell Studio, taken from the couple's deposit.

Your packages, your prices

List your own packages and prices, the same as on your own website, and set your own deposit (at least 20%).

Get seen

A flipbook portfolio, a “New on Joenell” spot, a welcome post on Instagram and 3 free blog and Instagram features every quarter.

What we ask

Our standards

  • One full gallery, in private. Send one complete wedding or elopement gallery. It's never published, it only shows you can cover a whole day.
  • A chapter portfolio. At least 5 photos for each moment: getting ready, ceremony, portraits, details and reception. Photos can come from different weddings.
  • Liability insurance. Many venues ask for it, so we do too.
  • A short meeting with Joenell, in person or on video.
  • Fast replies. Accept or decline booking requests within 48 hours.
  • The standard agreement for every Joenell Studio booking, and delivery on time.

Newer photographers are welcome. If you're not ready to lead full weddings yet, the second-shooter roster is a great way to build experience.
